Before you pay — how Rain Check Debate runs

Your cart includes Rain Check Debate. Please read the format carefully before continuing.

Three days, entirely by email, against a real opposing team.

Day 1 — The Case. The motion goes out and each team is told which side it argues. Teams have the day to write Draft One, their opening paper, 600–800 words. Submitted by end of day.
Day 2 — The Exchange. We pair the teams and email each team its opponent’s Draft One. Nothing is due today; teams read what they’re up against.
Day 3 — The Reply. Each team writes its Final Draft, 800–1,000 words, defending its own case and rebutting the opposition’s paper point by point. It must quote or reference specific arguments from that paper — that’s what stops it being a rewrite of Draft One.

Scoring: both drafts count — Draft One at 40%, Final Draft at 60% — so the weight sits on how well you argue back rather than how well you opened.

Good to know

Your questions, answered

When does registration open?

31 August 2026, 9:00 AM IST — the countdown marks the moment. This page unlocks fully then.

Who can take part?

School students across India, typically Classes 6–12. It’s online-first, so anyone can join on equal terms. Individuals and schools are both welcome.

How do I add multiple participants’ names?

You don’t enter names during payment. After your payment is confirmed, we’ll ask for the full name of every participant, exactly as it should appear on their certificate.

What do the competitions cost?

Science & Technology events are ₹600 per participant; Multidisciplinary events are ₹400 per participant. An event’s fee is the rate × the number of participants in your team. Schools entering 14+ events get 40% off the whole order.

Is payment secure?

Yes — payment is handled by Razorpay (cards, UPI, net-banking). Your card details are processed by Razorpay and never touch our servers.

Can I use a promo code?

Enter it in the cart and press Apply — your total updates before you pay.

What do I get?

Every participant who completes a competition gets a verifiable Certificate of Participation with a unique ID and QR code. Top performers earn Certificates of Achievement, medals and awards.

How will you contact me?

By email and WhatsApp using the details you provide, with schedules and instructions after you register.
