Good to know

Your questions, answered

When does registration open?

31 August 2026, 9:00 AM IST — the countdown marks the moment. This page unlocks fully then.

Who can take part?

School students across India, typically Classes 6–12. It’s online-first, so anyone can join on equal terms. Individuals and schools are both welcome.

How do I add multiple participants’ names?

You don’t enter names during payment. After your payment is confirmed, we’ll ask for the full name of every participant, exactly as it should appear on their certificate.

What do the competitions cost?

Science & Technology events are ₹600 per participant; Multidisciplinary events are ₹400 per participant. An event’s fee is the rate × the number of participants in your team. Schools entering 15+ events get 40% off the whole order.

Is payment secure?

Yes — payment is handled by Razorpay (cards, UPI, net-banking). Your card details are processed by Razorpay and never touch our servers.

Can I use a promo code?

Enter it in the cart and press Apply — your total updates before you pay.

What do I get?

Every participant who completes a competition gets a verifiable Certificate of Participation with a unique ID and QR code. Top performers earn Certificates of Achievement, medals and awards.

How will you contact me?

By email and WhatsApp using the details you provide, with schedules and instructions after you register.
