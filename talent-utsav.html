WHAT IS TALENT UTSAV

The festival that celebrates every kind of brilliant.

Talent Utsav is the grand annual festival of Inspire Talent Hub — a once-a-year celebration where the country’s most curious, creative and courageous students come together on a single, national stage. It is the finale our whole year builds towards.

Science and sport of the mind. Art and algorithms. Words, music, movement and ideas. Talent Utsav is built on one simple belief: every child is gifted at something — and every gift deserves a stage, an audience and a fair shot at the spotlight. This December, that stage gets bigger than ever.

Whether you code, sing, debate, paint, build or dream — there is an arena here with your name on it.

With 1,000+ schools expected from every corner of the country and a prize pool worth ₹50 lakh, Talent Utsav 2026 is set to be the biggest stage Indian students have ever shared.

WHY TALENT UTSAV

Built to be the biggest — and the fairest.

A truly national stage

Students from every state, every board and every kind of school compete side by side on one fair platform.

Fair, published judging

Every category is scored against clear, published criteria — no favourites, no black boxes, just merit.

Certificates you can verify

Every award carries a unique ID that anyone can verify online, forever. Recognition that actually holds weight.

A ₹50 lakh prize pool

Cash prizes, scholarships, gadgets and once-in-a-lifetime opportunities across every single category.

Open to every school

Fully accessible participation so talent decides who wins — not where you were born or which school you attend.

Mentorship & spotlight

Finalists are mentored, celebrated and given a platform that opens doors far beyond the competition itself.

HOW IT WORKS

From registration to the grand finale.

  1. Registrations open

    In 30 days, entries go live. Pick your categories and lock your spot — early birds get the best perks.

  2. Compete in your arenas

    Take part online and in regional rounds across your chosen categories, at your own pace.

  3. National finals

    The best from across the country advance to the national finals — the real test of the year.

  4. The Grand Finale · Talent Utsav

    A spectacular December celebration: live showcases, the ₹50 lakh prize pool and felicitation of India's finest young talent.
