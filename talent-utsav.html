WHY THIS EDITION MATTERS

The first year we bring every arena together.

Our seasonal competitions give students a chance to explore a subject they love. Talent Utsav brings those interests together for one national finale each year. 2026 is the first edition.

A chess player, a filmmaker and a robotics team can represent the same school, each in a category that fits their work. With more than 1,000 schools expected, this December’s finale is the largest competition Inspire Talent Hub has planned.

BEFORE YOU ENTER

Which Talent Utsav category fits you?

Start with something you already enjoy doing. If you sketch characters in your notebook, explore Art & Design. If you like bringing those characters to life on stage, look at Drama & Theatre. If your favourite part is telling their story, try Creative Writing.

For students: try a small version first

Record a minute of music, solve a timed maths problem or explain a prototype to a friend. Notice which part you want to try again. That is a useful clue when you compare festival categories.

For schools: build a shortlist

Ask students across Classes 6–12 what they would like to perform, build or present. Group their interests by arena, then check each category’s entry requirements when registration opens.

Looking for a competition outside the annual festival? Browse Inspire’s school competition arenas and past seasons.
