Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information — and the choices you have.

Last updated: July 2026

Inspire Talent Hub ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and the forms and tools available on it.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: when you submit an enquiry, registration interest, application, or subscription form, we collect the details you enter — such as your name, email, phone number, school, and message.
  • Local storage: after a registration payment, limited transaction details may be stored temporarily in your browser to display a confirmation, then cleared.
  • Technical data: standard information your browser shares (such as device and browser type) may be processed by the services that host and deliver this site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries, applications, and partnership or school requests.
  • To send updates you have asked for (for example, competition or resource announcements).
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website and our competitions.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third parties to deliver parts of this site, including form handling (form submissions are processed by our forms provider and emailed to us), web fonts, and content delivery. Payments, where applicable, are handled by a payment provider; we do not store full payment details. These providers process data under their own privacy terms.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal, mostly essential storage. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to manage it.

Data Retention & Security

We keep the information you submit only as long as needed to respond and operate our services, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Our competitions are aimed at school students, often participating with the involvement of parents, guardians, or schools. We collect only what is needed for participation and communication, and we encourage parental involvement for younger students.

Your Choices & Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you have submitted, or unsubscribe from updates, by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please contact us. We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
