The Methodology

Evaluation Process

A transparent, step-by-step breakdown of our rigorous three-stage evaluation framework.

1

Initial Screening (30% Weight)

Preliminary review to shortlist the most promising entries. The judging panel assesses:

  • Technical Accuracy & Concept Understanding
  • Innovation & Original Creativity
  • Presentation Quality & Structure
Outcome: Top projects advance to Expert Review.
2

Expert Review (40% Weight)

Detailed assessment by industry experts and educators examining depth and practicality:

  • Project Impact & Real-world Scalability
  • Implementation Feasibility
  • Technical Soundness & Logical Rationale
Outcome: The elite tier advances to Finals.
3

Final Round (30% Weight)

Comprehensive evaluation based on overall effectiveness, innovation, and execution:

  • Project Completeness & Effectiveness
  • Depth of Research & Analysis
  • Practical Application Readiness
Outcome: Final Winners are crowned and rewarded.
