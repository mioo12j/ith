Why trust a result

Fairness is engineered, not promised

A result is only worth having if the process behind it can withstand scrutiny. Here is what stands behind every placement.

Published criteria

The rubric is released before you compete, so you always know exactly what reviewers reward. No secret expectations, no moving goalposts.

Independent judging

Reviewers assess the work, not the name behind it — and they are calibrated against shared examples so similar work receives similar scores.

Verifiable certificates

Every certificate carries a unique ID and QR code, so a parent, teacher or admissions officer can confirm it independently, for years.

Holding a certificate? Check it in seconds. Enter the Certificate ID or scan its QR code to see the official participant, competition and issue date.

Answers

Questions people ask most

Who can participate in Inspire Talent Hub competitions?

Students across India, typically from Grades 6 to 12, can take part. Every competition page lists its exact eligibility, class range, and registration status.

Are the certificates recognised and verifiable?

Yes. Every participant and winner receives a certificate with a unique ID that can be validated online through our Certificate Verification tool.

How are competitions evaluated?

Submissions are assessed through transparent, criteria-based evaluation by experienced educators, with clear scoring methodology published for each competition. Our judging process explains it in full.

Is the Study Hub free to use?

Yes, completely. The CBSE & ICSE Study Hub runs in your browser for Classes 6 to 12 — chapter notes, flashcards, timed practice tests and printable exam papers with verified answer keys. No login required, and your progress stays on your own device.
