How It Works

Institutional Participation in Five Steps

Register Your School

Submit a short institutional enquiry and receive onboarding details from our team.

Assign a Coordinator

Nominate a teacher as your school coordinator to manage participation and communication.

Bulk Enrol Students

Register students in groups by class or category with a simple, streamlined process.

Compete Fairly

Students participate in transparent, criteria-based competitions across chosen categories.

Recognise & Report
