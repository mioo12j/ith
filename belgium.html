Why Belgium?

A Kingdom of Diversity

Where French elegance meets German precision, where Dutch-speaking Flanders blends with French-speaking Wallonia and a German-speaking east — Belgium is a small country with a big story. A crossroads of Latin Europe: art, food, innovation, culture and remarkably warm people.

Rich History World-Class Art & Museums Culinary Delights Innovation & Sustainability Warm People

Heritage

Explore Belgium’s Rich Heritage

From the past to the present — a journey of art, culture, cuisine and innovation.

Masterpieces & Painters

Jan van Eyck, Peter Paul Rubens and René Magritte — the Flemish Primitives to Surrealism.

Food & Beverages

Chocolate, waffles, fries and mussels, and over a thousand celebrated Belgian beers.

Architecture & Cities

Medieval Bruges, the Grand Place of Brussels, Ghent and cosmopolitan Antwerp.

Comics & Pop Culture

Tintin, The Smurfs, Lucky Luke — the beloved home of the ninth art.

Traditions & Festivals

Sinterklaas, the Carnival of Binche, local customs and gracious social etiquette.

Nature & Agriculture

Fertile Flemish fields, the Ardennes forests and a sustainable, future-facing land.
