BEFORE YOU SET OFF

A few good questions.

Who can participate?

Students in Classes 6–12, subject to the eligibility and format of each event.

Can I still enter Monsoon Minds 2026?

No. Registration closed on 30 September 2026. Visit the official championship page for current announcements.

Do I need to travel?

The competitions are online-first. Check the published instructions for your chosen event.

Where can I prepare?

The free CBSE & ICSE Study Hub has chapter notes, flashcards, practice tests and printable papers.

How do I verify a certificate?

Use the certificate ID on our verification page. The record should match the participant and competition details.

Verify a certificate ↗
What if I missed registration?

You can use Study Hub and Practice Arena while preparing for a future season. Check Events for confirmed announcements.
