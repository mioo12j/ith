For Parents & Guardians

A Parent’s Guide

Practical, reassuring guidance to help your child get the most out of competing — with confidence and without pressure.

Why It Helps

Growth Beyond the Report Card

Builds Confidence

Fair challenges and recognition help children believe in their abilities.

Encourages Growth

Feedback turns every attempt into a learning opportunity.

Develops Character

Discipline, resilience, and healthy ambition grow with practice.

How to Support

Encourage, Don’t Pressure

  • Focus on effort and learning, not only winning
  • Help your child read the criteria and plan their time
  • Celebrate participation and progress, whatever the result
  • Keep it enjoyable — curiosity lasts longer than pressure
  • Use performance feedback to encourage the next step
