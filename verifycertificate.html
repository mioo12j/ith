Authenticity Check

Verify a Certificate

Confirm that an Inspire Talent Hub certificate is genuine. Enter its Certificate ID below, or simply scan the QR code on the certificate — it opens this page and verifies automatically.

The Certificate ID is printed on the certificate, in the format ITH-YEAR-COMPETITION-CODE.

Point your camera at the QR code on the certificate.

In-page scanning isn’t supported on this browser. Just point your phone’s camera app at the certificate’s QR code — it opens this page and verifies automatically — or type the Certificate ID above.

How It Works

Trusted, Transparent Verification

Every certificate carries a unique ID and a QR code. Verification confirms the certificate was genuinely issued and shows its official details.

Unique IDs

No two certificates share an ID, so each one can be checked independently.

Scan or Type

Scan the QR code with any phone camera, or type the ID here.

Official Details

See the participant, competition, category and issue date on record.
