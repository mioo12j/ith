Using These Files

Guidance & Brand Usage

A few simple guidelines so our brand and materials are used consistently and fairly.

Logo Usage

Please keep the logo’s proportions and clear space intact, avoid recolouring or stretching it, and place it on backgrounds that keep it legible. Do not imply partnership or endorsement without our written agreement.

Competition Materials

Guidelines, category overviews, and evaluation criteria are provided for information. Rules for a specific competition always take precedence and are published on that competition’s page.

Certificates & Verification

Certificates are personal to each recipient. To confirm one is genuine, use the online verifier with the Certificate ID or scan its QR code - never rely on a screenshot alone.
