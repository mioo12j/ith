Helpful Tips

Why does it say "Record Not Found"?

To protect your privacy, we require an exact match. Make sure you typed your name exactly as you submitted it during registration, and double-check that you selected the correct competition.

Do I still get a certificate if I didn't place?

Absolutely! We celebrate everyone's hard work. If you participated, you will be able to download a beautiful Certificate of Participation here.

How Verification Works

Why should I verify a certificate?

To prevent forgery and ensure that achievements are accurately represented. When you enter a valid ID, our system checks it against our secure database and displays the original, untampered details of the award.
