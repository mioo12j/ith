Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and browser storage - and how you can control them.

Last updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Inspire Talent Hub uses cookies and similar browser storage on this website, and how you can control them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. "Local storage" is a related technology that lets a website keep small amounts of data in your browser. Together they help websites work and remember limited information.

How We Use Them

  • Essential: to make the site function and load reliably.
  • Functional (local storage): to briefly hold registration confirmation details so we can show your transaction summary, after which it is cleared; and to remember your Study Hub progress and chosen world appearance on your own device.
  • Third-party: services we rely on — such as web fonts and content delivery — may set their own cookies as part of delivering the site.

We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and clear local storage the same way. Blocking essential storage may affect how parts of the site work.

More Information

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information, or contact us with any questions.
