How this quiz works

Please read before you start

1
Enter your details & pick your level

Fill your name, team name, class, school and contact, then choose Junior or Senior — your questions depend on it.

2
Allow camera & microphone

When you press Start, your browser asks for camera and microphone. On a computer it may also ask to share your screen. These are recorded for integrity.

3
Answer within 30 minutes

One attempt only. A visible timer runs the whole time and cannot be paused. Use the number grid, Previous/Next and Flag. When the timer ends your answers submit automatically.

4
Review, then submit

On the last question press "Review & Submit" to see flagged / unanswered questions, then confirm. A recording file downloads to your device.

🚦

Rules

Violations are logged automatically and sent to the organisers

  • Single 30-minute attempt — it cannot be paused, restarted or done later. Refreshing does not reset it.
  • No notes, no search engines, no outside help and no second device (a second device is grounds for disqualification).
  • Copy, paste, right-click and developer tools are disabled. Leaving the tab/window is logged.
  • Marking: +4 for a correct answer, -1 for a wrong answer, 0 for blank. It can be safer to leave a question blank than to guess wildly.
  • Covers geography, history, science, sport and India/world facts.
  • Each question has exactly one correct option.
🎥

Your session is recorded

Please read this carefully

⚠️
The recording is required — keep it safe

A watermarked video of your session (camera, and your screen on a computer) is recorded and downloads to your own device at the end as an INSPIRE_AUDIT_…webm file. It carries your name, reference number and a moving timestamp, so it cannot be quietly altered. Please keep this file for at least 7 days. If your result is ever questioned and the recording is not available, that is grounds for disqualification. By starting, you accept that you will be recorded.
